# PRD review lenses

## Problem fit
- Solution disguised as requirement
- Pain not evidenced
- Success criteria missing or unmeasurable

## Users & scenarios
- Missing roles
- Missing frequency / context
- Happy-path only

## Scope
- Non-goals absent
- MVP bloated
- Hidden must-haves in "later"

## Rules & states
- Conflicts without priority
- Undefined states
- Exceptions missing

## Interaction
- No failure/empty/loading behavior
- High interaction cost unexplained

## Testability
- "智能/适当/尽量" without meaning
- Acceptance criteria not observable

## Assumptions
- Critical assumptions unmarked
- Decisions claimed "confirmed" without evidence

## Tech leakage
- DB/framework/class design written as product req
- Algorithm internals instead of user-visible behavior

## Consistency
- Conflicts with existing product behavior undocumented
